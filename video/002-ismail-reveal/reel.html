With gratitude to Allah
A story of patience,
prayer & a promise kept
Prophet Ibrahim (a.s.)
For many long years,
he had no child.
Through hardship and waiting,
he never stopped praying.
He raised his hands and said:
رَبِّ هَبْ لِى مِنَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ
“My Lord, grant me [a child]
from among the righteous.”
Surah As-Saffat · 37:100
And Allah answered him:
فَبَشَّرْنَٰهُ بِغُلَٰمٍ حَلِيمٍۢ
“So We gave him good tidings
of a forbearing boy.”
Surah As-Saffat · 37:101
In his old age, Ibrahim (a.s.) praised his Lord:
ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِى وَهَبَ لِى عَلَى ٱلْكِبَرِ إِسْمَٰعِيلَ وَإِسْحَٰقَ ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّى لَسَمِيعُ ٱلدُّعَآءِ
“Praise to Allah, who has granted me in old age
Ismail and Isaac. Indeed, my Lord
is the Hearer of supplication.”
Surah Ibrahim · 14:39
Ismail (a.s.) · Son of Ibrahim (a.s.)
وَٱذْكُرْ فِى ٱلْكِتَٰبِ إِسْمَٰعِيلَ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ صَادِقَ ٱلْوَعْدِ وَكَانَ رَسُولًۭا نَّبِيًّۭا
“And mention in the Book, Ismail. Indeed, he was
true to his promise, and he was a messenger
and a prophet.”
